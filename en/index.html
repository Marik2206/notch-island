FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Download it, use it and share it with your classmates.

Why does macOS show a warning on first launch?

The app is not notarized by Apple yet (that requires a paid developer account), so macOS shows its standard warning. You confirm it once and after that the island starts normally.

Which permissions does it ask for, and why?

Calendar, to show events next to your classes. Location, to show the weather where you are. Automation, to read music from Spotify and Apple Music and to close distracting tabs in Focus mode. You can decline any of them and everything else keeps working.

Does it work on a Mac without a notch?

It is designed for MacBooks with a notch. On other Macs it still appears at the top center of the screen, but it looks best around a real notch.

Which music players does it support?

Spotify, Apple Music, Yandex Music and anything that shows up in the system Now Playing widget, including music in your browser.
